\textbf{Invariant fuzzing} (\texttt{code/fuzz.py}; invariants I1--I5 of paper \S2). Each run is VM, FR or FF on the real Branch rules with, from a seed: launch loss uniform in 0--35\% per launch; duplicate batches (up to 50\%, replayed up to 30 h later); reordering (application delivery held back up to 30 h); a contradictory LOCK for a decided deal; 0--2 endpoint resets; the offer holder's local withdrawal (30\% of runs); and a \emph{crash right after ledger step $k$} (the reply that follows the durable record is never sent and that Branch resets), $k$ enumerated over every ledger step of the product. After every ledger step the ledger asserts conservation, no unit in two uses and every claim covered; at the end, every lock is closed, each deal ID has one recorded decision, the holder's outcome agrees with it, every Branch's claims equal its backing, and nothing is encumbered.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{fuzz_summary.tex}\end{center}
Seeds are $0\ldots\FuzzPerProduct-1$ per product; a failing run prints its seed. This is \emph{evidence, not proof}: it covers timing, loss, duplication, order, resets and crash points, not every interleaving, and no Branch breaks a rule (S2 tests that separately). Three simulator errors were found and fixed (paper \S2).

\section*{E2. Probability and traffic}

\textbf{Direct service.} No rule uses it for coordination (R13); a direct Ceres$\to$Jupiter print copy (4.06 AU) would be lost with probability $1-e^{-0.08\times4.06} = 27.7\%$.

\textbf{Backbone, per launch (exact) and per hop (bound).} Each launch uses $p = 1 - e^{-0.02d}$ from its own
geometry. A hop is abandoned only if all four launches are lost, bounded by $p_{\max}^4$ over the link's launches.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_probabilities.tex}\end{center}
An endpoint attempt is lost if any hop is abandoned; four lost attempts leave \emph{status unknown}. With no incident
attempts are over 24 h apart and share no window or queue, so we multiply them (checked: $0.548^2 = 30.0\%$, $0.0333^4 = 1.2\times10^{-6}$ agree with the
Monte Carlo); in S2 the isolation makes attempts 1--3 fail together, and we do not. \textbf{Financial effect of an abandoned hop:} none
(R6, R8).

\textbf{Completion probability at all nine settlements} (\texttt{code/e2\_latency.py}, \EtwoN\ runs per row, seed \EtwoSeed, no incident;
95\% half-width $\le \EtwoCI$ percentage points on every entry; entries above 99.995\% are shown $>$99.99, not 100). A failed launch costs one $R_h$;
four abandon the attempt; attempts are enqueued every $R_e$; the deal commits on the first LOCK arrival before its 96 h expiry. DvP is with Neptune (Neptune trades with Earth); the future's settlement is the SETTLE from Ceres.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_latency.tex}\end{center}
The far trade completes on time only \EtwoVMEarthNoLoss\% of the time (the Neptune hop loses 42.7\% of launches, each retry waits $R_h = 8.7$ h), and Neptune's future settlement is the weakest (55.2\% on time). It is declined (funded: the lock
returns) in \EtwoVMEarthDecline\% of runs from Earth and \EtwoVMUranusDecline\% from Uranus under R5. \textbf{Offer life:} against a 48 h
offer the same deliveries are declined \EtwoVMEarthDeclineFortyEight\% (Earth), \EtwoVMUranusDeclineFortyEight\% (Uranus); against 24 h,
\EtwoVMEarthDeclineTwentyFour\% and \EtwoVMUranusDeclineTwentyFour\%.

\textbf{Sensitivity to loss and timer} (same Monte Carlo; loss law $1-e^{-0.02md}$ on every launch; $R_e$ is fixed by the brief, so its rows show exposure, not a choice):
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_sweep.tex}\end{center}
At four times the stated loss R5 is no longer enough: \EtwoSweepLossFourDecline\% of Earth trades are declined, \EtwoSweepLossFourUranusDecline\% from Uranus, and a 24 h completion falls to \EtwoSweepLossFourDay\%. The
design is tuned to the brief's law; a different law needs a longer offer life (R5 is a formula, not a constant).

\textbf{Quota and capacity.} Peak per rolling 24 h: 2 of the shared 600 and 2 of one Branch's 66 (\SimSTwoPeakBranch\ in S2-FR); no link queue holds more than 3 of 10{,}000; at most 2 of 64 data packets unacknowledged. The one known wait is the h 240 print, held 24 h by B--Ceres maintenance.
