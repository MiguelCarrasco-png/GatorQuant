\section*{E2. Probability and traffic}

\textbf{Direct service.} No rule uses it (paper R13): clients use local access and prints cross on the backbone, so
no reported time depends on it. For comparison, a direct Ceres$\to$Jupiter print copy (4.06 AU at h 0) is lost with
probability $1-e^{-0.08\times4.06} = 27.7\%$; copies are not independent under an isolation that hits them all.

\textbf{Backbone, per launch (exact) and per hop (bound).} Each launch uses $p = 1 - e^{-0.02d}$ from its own
geometry. A hop is abandoned only if all four launches are lost, bounded by $p_{\max}^4$ over the link's launches.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_probabilities.tex}\end{center}
An endpoint attempt is lost if any hop is abandoned; four lost attempts leave \emph{status unknown}. With no incident
attempts are over 24 h apart and share no window or queue, so we multiply them; in S2 the isolation makes attempts
1--3 fail together, and we do not. \textbf{Financial effect of an abandoned hop:} none; the lock or pledge stays (R6),
the endpoint retries, and status unknown triggers resubmission (R8).

\textbf{Completion probability under random loss} (\texttt{code/e2\_latency.py}, $10^6$ runs per row, no incident).
Each launch is an independent trial; a failed launch costs one $R_h$; four abandon the attempt; attempts are enqueued
every $R_e$; the deal commits on the first LOCK arrival before expiry.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_latency.tex}\end{center}
The far trade completes at its no-loss time only \EtwoVMEarthNoLoss\% of the time, because the Neptune hop loses
42.7\% of launches and each retry waits $R_h = 8.7$ h. It is declined (funded: the lock returns) in
\EtwoVMEarthDecline\% of runs from Earth and \EtwoVMUranusDecline\% from Uranus under R5 (offer life 96 h).
\textbf{Sensitivity to offer life:} the same deliveries against a 48 h offer are declined \EtwoVMEarthDeclineFortyEight\%
(Earth) and \EtwoVMUranusDeclineFortyEight\% (Uranus); against 24 h, \EtwoVMEarthDeclineTwentyFour\% and
\EtwoVMUranusDeclineTwentyFour\%. The future's links are short: its settlement is spendable within 6 h of the
no-loss time in \EtwoSettleSixH\% of runs.

\textbf{Quota and capacity.} Peak use per rolling 24 h: 2 of the shared 600 and 2 of one Branch's 66; no link queue
holds more than 3 of 10{,}000; at most 2 of 64 data packets unacknowledged. The one known wait is the h 240 print,
held 24 h by B--Ceres maintenance.

\section*{E3. Balances and capital}

Opening sheet (authoritative at h 0; source: declared; owner and location as listed; no liabilities or encumbrances),
then every ledger line that changes, in time order, with the balance after and what is encumbered for which deal. A
\emph{claim} line is a Branch's liability to its client, backed by its inter-Branch holding or a recorded pending
release. S2 is FF with the last line at h \SimSTwoSpendable\ instead of h 288.6895. After every step the simulator
asserts: NeoDollar and share totals equal the opening; no balance is negative; encumbrance never exceeds balance (no
asset serves two uses); claims are covered by holdings plus pending releases; each pending release is backed by an
encumbrance. \SimChecks\ checks over \SimRuns\ runs passed.
