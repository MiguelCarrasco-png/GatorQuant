\section*{E2. Probability and traffic}

\textbf{Direct service.} No rule uses it (paper R13): every client is at its own Branch's settlement and talks to
it by local access, and prints reach remote Branches over the backbone. So no reported time depends on the direct
service and there is no direct success probability to give. For comparison, a direct copy of a print from Ceres to
Jupiter (4.06 AU at h 0) is lost with probability $1-e^{-0.08\times4.06} = 27.7\%$ with no retry; copies cannot be multiplied as
independent under an isolation that hits them all.

\textbf{Backbone, per launch and per hop (exact per launch; bound per hop).} Every launch of every run uses
$p = 1 - e^{-0.02d}$ from its own geometry. A hop is abandoned only if all four of its launches are lost; we bound
this by $p_{\max}^4$ over all launches on that link.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_probabilities.tex}\end{center}
An endpoint attempt is lost if any hop is abandoned; four attempts all lost leaves \emph{status unknown}. With no
incident the attempts are more than 24 h apart and share no window or queue, so we multiply them; in S2 the
isolation makes attempts 1--3 fail together, and we do not. \textbf{Financial effect of an abandoned hop:} none.
The lock or pledge stays (R6), the endpoint retries, and status unknown triggers resubmission (R8).

\textbf{Completion probability under random loss} (\texttt{code/e2\_latency.py}, $10^6$ Monte Carlo runs per row,
no incident). Each launch is an independent trial; a failed launch costs one $R_h$; four failures abandon the
attempt; attempts are enqueued every $R_e$; the deal commits on the first LOCK arrival before expiry.
\begin{center}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{e2_latency.tex}\end{center}
The far trade completes at its no-loss time only \EtwoVMEarthNoLoss\% of the time, because the Neptune hop loses
42.7\% of launches and each retry waits $R_h = 8.7$ h. It is declined (funded: the lock returns) in
\EtwoVMEarthDecline\% of runs from Earth and \EtwoVMUranusDecline\% from Uranus under R5 (offer life 96 h).
\textbf{Sensitivity to offer life:} the same deliveries against a 48 h offer are declined \EtwoVMEarthDeclineFortyEight\%
(Earth) and \EtwoVMUranusDeclineFortyEight\% (Uranus); against 24 h, \EtwoVMEarthDeclineTwentyFour\% and
\EtwoVMUranusDeclineTwentyFour\%. The future's links are
short, so its settlement is spendable within 6 h of the no-loss time in \EtwoSettleSixH\% of runs.

\textbf{Quota and capacity.} Peak use of the shared 600 is 2 per rolling 24 h; of one Branch's 66-packet slice, 2;
no link queue ever holds more than 3 of its 10{,}000 packets; at most 2 of 64 data packets are unacknowledged. The one
known wait is the h 240 print held 24 h by B--Ceres maintenance.

\section*{E3. Balances and capital}

Opening sheet (authoritative at h 0; source: declared; owner and location as listed; no liabilities; nothing
encumbered). Below it, every ledger line that changes, in time order, with the balance after and what is encumbered
and for which deal. A \emph{claim} line is a liability of the named Branch to its client, backed by that Branch's
inter-Branch holding or by a recorded pending release. S2 is FF with the last line at h \SimSTwoSpendable\ instead of
h 288.6895. After every step the simulator asserts: total NeoDollars and shares equal the opening totals; no balance
is negative; encumbrance never exceeds balance (no asset serves two uses); every claim is covered by holdings plus
pending releases; every pending release is backed by an encumbrance. \SimChecks\ checks over \SimRuns\ runs passed.
