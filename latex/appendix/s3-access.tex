\section*{S3. Access at every settlement}

Every settlement has a Branch and every product. A cross-planet trade needs the counterparty's Branch (here Neptune);
the Ceres Iron future needs the referee and price source at Ceres. Best route = fastest simple route of at most three
links that can launch now; availability = share of the next 24 h in which every link of it can launch (geometry and
maintenance, 3-minute samples). Columns per authority: route, one-way delay (h) and availability (\%) at h 0 and h 300.
{\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\gen{s3_access.tex}}

Ranked by the h 0 round trip to Neptune, the best settlement is Ceres (\SimCeresComplete h), the median Venus (\SimVenusComplete h, 4th of 8) and the worst Uranus (\SimUranusComplete h): VM re-run from reset with Terra Capital and its \$150{,}000 at that settlement; no capital is added. Uranus is slowest because its link to Relay A is 16.8 AU and Neptune's 27.9 AU; its Relay A launches also lose 28.5\% each (E2).
