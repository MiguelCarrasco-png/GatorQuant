\section*{E5. Shifted epochs (Tier 3 claimed)}

Each run starts at $t = k \times 365.25$ d with planets and relays advanced and balances reset to the opening sheet; maintenance and the S2 incident are not replayed; prints and maturity
shift with the run (\texttt{code/e5\_epochs.py}); sessions open 72 h ahead on the foresight route. \textbf{Tier 1.} At +1, +10 and +100 years both scenarios complete with no suspension;
delays move with geometry (value move 8.65--9.61 h; the future settles 0.76--1.46 h after its print); +100 y pins via Relay B.

\textbf{Tier 2: the difficult epoch.} Of the 54 closures of a Jupiter or Ceres relay link in E4's 200 years we placed a run so that one hits the future's pinned route between the last interim print and the settling print, making SETTLE the first message to meet it: h \EfiveHardH (+\EfiveHardY y; \EfiveHardDate), Jupiter--Relay A closes at run hour \EfiveClosureStart, 6 h before the print, for \EfiveClosureDays days (\EfiveClosureH h, beyond the 30-day packet lifetime). The rising run is harder: Callisto, the long at Jupiter, is paid only when SETTLE crosses the closed link.

\textbf{Tier 3: both directions.} R16 sees the closure before SETTLE is sent (h \EfiveRerouteAt), opens a fresh session via Relay B, and SETTLE is spendable at h \EfiveFRDone,
\EfiveLate\ h after the open-route time, in both runs; queuing behind the closure, as the brief's waiting rule alone would, holds SETTLE \EfiveNaiveLate\ h with \EfiveNaiveLaunches\ launches.
\textbf{Funded consequences:} nothing is suspended; the cost is \EfiveExtraUsdH\ extra locked \$-hours (the \$75{,}000 pledge $\times$ \EfiveLate\ h) and one quota packet; the table's
\EfiveTotalUsdH\ is that on top of the open-route baseline of \EfiveBaseUsdH.
