\section{Deployment assessment}\label{sec:deploy}

The baseline omits real effects on purpose. Because every obligation is locked before it exists, an effect that
delays, loses or reorders messages can change \emph{when} a deal completes, never \emph{whether} it is funded. The two that could
change \emph{which} outcome settles (a deadline on the wrong clock, a print that cannot arrive) are answered by R17 and R10/R12.
``Derived'' marks our own estimate from the cited formula with circular, coplanar orbits: order of magnitude only.

\textbf{Where our assumptions stop.} Orbits are fixed Kepler ellipses, which drift from the real solar system over decades; E4 and E5 are claims about the model. We assume Branches follow their rules (the brief's baseline); R22 \extension \ detects three kinds of rule-breaking, not a Branch that withholds a decision, and we design no enforcement. We assume durable storage never loses a recorded decision (else G2 fails for that deal). A Branch gone for good, and a forged Price Board print, are outside the baseline: locks stay and every claim stays covered, but nothing recovers them.
