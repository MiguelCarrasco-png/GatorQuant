\section{Risks we found and how we treat them}\label{sec:risks}

Because every obligation is locked in full before it exists, no risk below can make a balance wrong or leave a
winner unpaid (G1--G3). What remains is risk to \emph{service}: how long money stays locked, whether a deal happens at
all, and who sees prices first. We rank by the worst effect we measured on locked capital and lost service. Every
rank-1 to rank-5 risk was found by a run that made it bind; most come from an interaction between two of our own
rules or between a rule and the transport.

\textbf{Interactions we found between rules.}
(a) \emph{Pull rule $\times$ endpoint timer} (fixed). The timer-only pull waited one $R_e$ after reconnection: 6.1 h in S2, up to 36 h on far pairs. Resubmitting on contact alone does nothing there, since nobody sends in that window; what cut the lag to \SimSTwoFRSpendableAfter\ h was resubmitting every $P = 2.4$ h, the pace the 10-packet reserve affords (4.8 h costs no time, 9.6 h loses 3 h; S2).
(b) \emph{Pinning $\times$ waiting for known closures $\times$ 30-day lifetime.} The brief lets a sender wait out a known closure and the session is pinned, so a SETTLE parks behind a 51.8-day closure (risk 2). R16 breaks the chain.
(c) \emph{Offer expiry $\times$ endpoint and hop timers} (risk 4): 24 h and 32.4 h, each sensible, made the first retry useless; our first fix (live $T_0 + R_e$) still failed \EtwoVMEarthDeclineFortyEight\% of the time because it ignored hop retries.
(d) \emph{Reverse-link closure $\times$ hop receipt.} A packet launched just before its reverse link closes arrives but its receipt cannot return, so the sender retries into a closed link for up to four $R_h$ (E4, Earth: 49.6 min window); R16 forbids such launches.
(e) \emph{Freeze $\times$ maintenance} (FR-full). The freeze starts at h 264, exactly when B--Ceres maintenance ends, so Jupiter takers see the h 240 print only after it; accepted, since no money depends on it.
(f) \emph{Reset $\times$ handshake timer $\times$ forced loss} (found by the stacked scan). A reset Branch must handshake and the SYN's retry waits one $R_e$; a 6 h loss at the reconnection instant kills all four hop launches, so R8's pace cannot help until the peer opens its own session. That fresh-session rule cut the stack from 386.8 h to \SimStackSpendable\ h; the residual is one handshake round trip.
