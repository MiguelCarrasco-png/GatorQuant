\begin{abstract}
We run the exchange as nine \emph{Branches}, one per settlement, each keeping the only ledger for the assets held
there; there is no hub and no central bank. Nothing is owed across light-lag unless it is locked first: a cross-planet
trade locks the buyer's money at home, the seller's Branch decides once and commits, and the buyer's Branch releases
on the commit; a capped Ceres Iron future locks each side's full maximum loss at home and is settled by the Branch
where its price is published. No timer ever ends a lock, and only the lock holder resends, so a lost, late, duplicated
or contradictory message can cost time but never money (guarantees G1--G3, resting on rules alone). Completion is
conditional only on the two Branches eventually exchanging a packet, which on the common model is always possible:
a 200-year scan certified to 1 ms finds no settlement ever cut off. On the traced runs an Earth--Neptune share trade
completes in \SimVMComplete\ h, the 288-hour future pays in full in both price directions at h \SimFutSpendable\ with
30\% of all cash locked, and a 72 h isolation of the referee at maturity delays payment by \SimSTwoLost\ h without
touching a balance.
\end{abstract}
